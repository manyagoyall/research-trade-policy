# Trade Policy Uncertainty & U.S.-China FDI: 
This project tests whether trade policy uncertainty becoming more unpredictable is associated with lower foreign investment between the U.S. and China, using annual data from 2015-2024.

**What it does:**  It combines trade policy uncertainty index ( TPU) with U.S.-China
investment data (FDI), and calculates how much uncertainty has been swinging around
year to year. It then  runs a regression to test whether that swinging predicts lower investment.

**Data:** FRED Trade Policy Uncertainty Index; BEA bilateral FDI flows (U.S.-China)

**Finding:** Higher policy volatility is associated with lower investment
